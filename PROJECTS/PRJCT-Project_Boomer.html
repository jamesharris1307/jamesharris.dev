Project Boomer

Enjoy

This project is a retro 2.5d fps shooter with modern mechanics. The aesthetics definitely take inspiration from Wolfenstein 3D and the RavenSoftware games.

In this page you can see all of my dev logs and discussions surrounding the development of this project. All the challenges and resources I used in order to create it.

Dev Log #1

Inspiration is the first thing that grabs you and thrusts you into a project. For this project my inspiration came from a few places. The first thing I can remember grabbing my attention was the game Cultic. I immediately bought the game and instead of working on my final year University projects, I played this game for a couple of hours. I loved it. I loved how simple the enemies looked but how much detail was hidden away and left to your imagination. I loved how the colour all complemented each other. I'd previously been used to just Unreal Engine ultra realistic games that were starting to get repetitive and boring. I was totally fascinated by these older games and my imagination started to get out of control. That's when I stumbled across 2 youtube channels: Civvie11 and BrutalZlim These channels showed me all the cool games made back in the day. I watched a lot of videos and kept hearing the same 2 words being repeated over and over again . . .

BOOMER SHOOTER

I found it. The games I had been looking for. I was born in 2002 in the UK and as a child, I wasn't even allowed to watch power rangers cause it was too violent so as you can imagine I was not allowed to play Doom, Heretic, Duke Nukem, etc. As time went on and I got older those games stopped being talked about as much and new games like Far Cry 3 and 4, Saints Row 3, GTA V, MW3 came out and those are the games I grew up playing. So I hadn't had much experience playing boomer shooters.

It was around this point that I stumbled across a book called Masters of Doom. For christmas that year I was gifted a Kindle by my mum. After setting it up I browsed the store for some books to read. I hadn't read much up to this point. The last time I properly read a book was in my first year of Uni when I read Richard mattesons I am Legend (great book btw). I stumbled across Masters of Doom after asking ChatGPT to give me some book recommendations (at least Ai is good for something). From the first chapter of the book I was hooked. I went through phases of reading lots of the book every day to days where I didn't touch my kindle until I finished the book while sitting on the sofa. After finishing the book, my drive to create a Boomer Shooter was cemented.

This boomer shooter project at the moment doesn't have a story, name or visual style yet. But it does takes inspiration from Wolfenstein 3D, Half Life 2 and a few Doom mods. No we have reached the fun part.
