Frank Spencer Code

When well intentioned code behaves unexpectedly

What is Frank Spencer code ?

Frank Spencer is a character from a TV show that ran from 1973 to 1978 called some mothers do ave em. The character is a maladroit who frequently gets into trouble even though he's trying his best.

The problem with maladroit code (that is code that is accident prone and clumsy) is that it causes unexpected behaviour.

Manuel!!

Misunderstanding

Who is Manuel ?
and whats he got to do with Ai writing code

Manuel is a very funny character from the british TV show Fawlty Towers. When I was a kid I used to watch this show over and over again and laugh every time. I recently went to see the theatre production of the show in Cardiff at the Wales Millennium Centre.

While watching and laughing I made the comparison between manuel's character and Ai. Might seem like a bit of a stretch but watch the video below and then read on.

No matter how many times you try and explain what you want it to do for you, it almost always fails in some way. The best type of way for it to fail in its endeavour to complete the prompt you set it is drastically.

In the show one of the characters Manuel frequently and comically gets things wrong. Basil gives him a job to do and he hilariously messes it up somehow. The prompt basic gives can be very simple. Something most people would be able to do without any hassle. The only problem with Manuel is that he doesn't speak the language he is being prompted in and also he is portrayed as having little common sense. Lets explore then what it means to be fluent in a language and also have common sense.

A programmers wife asks him to go to the store. She says, Buy a loaf of bread, if they have eggs, buy a dozen. The programmer goes to the store and comes back with a dozen loafs of bread.

I've heard that breaking down a joke makes the joke less funny. Let's do it anyway.

The programmer breaks down the task into small components. Number 1 is to go to the store Number 2 is to buy the bread Number 3 is to check if they have eggs Number 4 is If they do buy a dozen loafs of bread. That's how a computer interprets instructions. It does exactly what its told
